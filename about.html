About TaskFlow

TaskFlow is a lightweight, modern task-management web application designed to help teams and individuals organize their work efficiently.

How to Use TaskFlow

  • Dashboard: Get a quick overview of your workspace, including total, pending, and completed task summaries.
  • Add Task: Create a new task by entering a title, description, due date, and priority level.
  • Task List: View all your ongoing and completed tasks in a single organized table. Use filters to quickly find what you need.
  • Task Detail: Click on any specific task to view its full details, mark it as completed, or delete it entirely.
